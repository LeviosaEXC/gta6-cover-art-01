NEON COAST

GTA-style cover maker — four panels, one night, one coastal city.

Drag a panel to move it. Drag the photo inside its frame to crop. Scroll to zoom. Tilt buttons rotate the panel. Drag a panel by its bottom edge to swap panels.

Snapshots

Each snapshot fills its own panel: 1 goes to panel 1, 2 to panel 2, 3 to panel 3, 4 to panel 4. One photo can fill several panels, or use four different photos.

Live preview. Drag inside a photo to pan its crop, scroll over a photo to zoom it, drag a panel by its bottom edge to swap panels.
